KANJIDIC2 provides the top-100 kanji selection, newspaper frequency ranks, and
reading verification. JMdict provides verification of example words, readings,
and meanings. The app's curated selection, condensed meanings, explanations,
and curriculum arrangement are adaptations. The dictionary-derived curriculum
in assets/curriculum/ is distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0. This notice does not
change the separate license of the application's software.

The 100-kanji ordering reflects the newspaper-frequency data distributed by
KANJIDIC2, not a universal ranking of everyday spoken Japanese. Only the
explicitly taught reading is assessed; Japanese characters can have other
correct readings in other contexts.
